How is this calculated?
ACWR (45%) — Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio. Compares this week's load to your 4-week average. Above 1.5 is the "danger zone" in sports science research.
Monotony (25%) — doing the same effort every day with no variation. High monotony means no recovery stimulus.
Consecutive days (15%) — more than 4 days running without rest.
Volume spike (15%) — week-on-week increase above 30%.

How does the Riegel formula work?
T2 = T1 × (D2/D1)^1.06 — where T1 is your reference time over D1 km, and T2 is the predicted time over D2 km. The exponent 1.06 accounts for the fact that running gets disproportionately harder at longer distances. We then adjust for your current fitness trend (recent vs historical volume).
